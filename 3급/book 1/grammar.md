### **Lesson 1: Introduction**
*   **-기는요:** To humbly negate or disagree with what someone has said.
*   **-을/ㄹ 뿐이다:** To state that something is "only" or "just" the case, with nothing more.
*   **-기 위해서:** To express the purpose or intent for doing something ("in order to").
*   **-는/은/ㄴ 편이다:** To describe something in relative terms, avoiding a definitive statement ("to be on the relatively... side").

### **Lesson 2: School Life**
*   **-으려던/려던 참이었다:** To express that you were just about to do something.
*   **-기만 하다:** To indicate that someone "only" does that action and nothing else.
*   **-어야지요/아야지요/여야지요:** To express that someone "must" or "should" do something.
*   **-어/아/여 보이다:** Used with a descriptive verb to say someone/something "looks" a certain way.

### **Lesson 3: Home**
*   **-는다면/ㄴ다면/다면:** To make a hypothetical supposition about something ("if... were to happen").
*   **-어서/아서/여서 그런지:** To suggest a possible reason for something ("Perhaps it's because...").
*   **피동 (Passive Voice):** The passive voice, where the subject is acted upon (e.g., 보이다, 쌓이다, 잡히다).
*   **-어/아/여 있다:** To indicate the state that remains after an action has been completed.

### **Lesson 4: Daily Life**
*   **-을지/ㄹ지:** To express thoughts about choosing between options ("whether to... or...").
*   **-어/아/여 놓다:** To indicate an action is completed and its result is being maintained for later use.
*   **-는대요/ㄴ대요/대요:** The shortened form for reported speech ("He/she said...").
*   **아무:** Used with ~나/나 to mean "anyone," "anything," "anytime," etc.

### **Lesson 5: Food and Cooking**
*   **-었더니/았더니/였더니:** To state a realization or outcome resulting from a past action.
*   **-잖아요:** To remind or confirm something the listener already knows ("As you know...").
*   **-고 나서:** To indicate that one action happens "after" another is completed.
*   **-는다면서요/ㄴ다면서요/다면서요?:** To ask for confirmation about something you heard ("Is it true that...?").

### **Lesson 6: Hospital**
*   **-는데도/은데도/ㄴ데도:** To express "even though" or "despite" something.
*   **-기가 쉽다:** Attached to a verb to mean "it is easy to..." (e.g., 걸리기가 쉬워요 - It's easy to catch).
*   **-을지도/ㄹ지도 모르다:** To express uncertainty or possibility ("might" or "may").
*   **-으려면/려면²:** Used with verbs of change to plan for a future point in time ("In order for it to be...").

### **Lesson 7: Cultural Pastime**
*   **이라든가/라든가:** Used when listing examples ("... or something like that").
*   **-었을/았을/였을 것이다:** To make a speculation about a past event ("must have," "probably").
*   **-나/은가/ㄴ가 보다:** To make an assumption based on visual evidence ("It looks like...," "I guess...").
*   **-는다고/ㄴ다고/다고 생각하다:** To formally state one's opinion ("I think that...").

### **Lesson 8: Lifestyle Habits**
*   **-는 대신에:** To indicate doing one thing "instead of" another, or "in return for" something.
*   **-게 하다:** The causative form, meaning "to make/let someone do something."
*   **-으면서도/면서도:** To express a contrast between two actions or states by the same subject ("even while...").
*   **-던데:** To provide background information based on personal experience before giving advice, making a suggestion, or asking a question.

### **Lesson 9: Problem and Solution**
*   **-고서:** To indicate a sequence of actions, emphasizing the first action is done before the second ("and then").
*   **-을/ㄹ 뻔하다:** To express that something almost happened ("nearly," "almost").
*   **사동 (Causative):** The causative form, where the subject makes someone else perform an action (e.g., 먹이다, 재우다).
*   **-으니까/니까²:** To state that the second clause was discovered or realized as a result of the action in the first clause.

### **Lesson 10: Mistakes and Apologies**
*   **-는다는/ㄴ다는 것이:** To express that something contrary to one's intention happened ("I was trying to... but...").
*   **-고 말다:** To express that something ended up happening, often regrettably or unintentionally ("ended up V-ing").
*   **-거든요:** Used at the end of a sentence to provide a reason or explanation for a previous statement.
*   **-을까/ㄹ까 봐:** To express worry or concern that something might happen ("I'm afraid that...").
